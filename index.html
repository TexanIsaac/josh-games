HOW TO PLAY

The idea

You are a noob with nothing but your fists. Zombies bite you, and if they bite you enough you turn into one. Then you bite noobs instead. You can end up on either side, over and over, and there is no game over.

Getting better

Every kill counts. At 7 kills you get a knife, at 22 a gun, and at 37 a riot shield that nothing can bite through. Turn into a zombie and the same four ranks are waiting on that side too.

Moving about

Left thumb to walk, HIT to fight. Drag anywhere else to swing the camera round. Walk into a wall to climb it, and jump down off the top.

The rows at the bottom

The top row is what you can turn into. Kill one of something and you can become it. The bottom row is your things: grenades, bombs, a tank, a bombing run. Tap one to use it.

Becoming the boss

Points build up as you fight. Get enough and BOSS lights up. Kill a boss and you unlock it for yourself.

Coins

Kills and finished waves pay coins, and they are kept for good. Spend them in the shop on hats, faces, weapons and upgrades.
